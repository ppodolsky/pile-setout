All calculations run in the browser; no data is sent anywhere. Method: non-linear least squares over the measurements (trilateration) + a search for the rigid placement of the design field that needs the fewest moves (for each angle, an exact solution of the tolerance-circle coverage problem). Minimality is confirmed either by a lower bound from conflicting pairs or by an exhaustive angle search; if the check does not finish, the plan is shown marked "optimality not confirmed". Each pile is moved at most once.

Corner-casting dimensions per ISO 668 (Annex A): S/P for 10', 20' and 40' = 2787/5853/11985 × 2259 mm. Sources: ISO 668:2020, William Cook — ISO corner castings. Intermediate supports (including the middle supports of the 20' field) are an editable starting point: before pouring concrete, check their contact with the longitudinal beams of the actual container (measure the gap with a feeler gauge with the container resting on its corner castings).
