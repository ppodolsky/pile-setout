All calculations run in the browser; no data is sent anywhere. Method: non-linear least squares over the measurements (trilateration) + a search for the rigid placement of the design field that needs the fewest moves (for each angle, an exact solution of the tolerance-circle coverage problem). Minimality is confirmed either by a lower bound from conflicting pairs or by an exhaustive angle search; if the check does not finish, the plan is shown marked "optimality not confirmed". Each pile is moved at most once.

Corner-casting dimensions per ISO 668 (Annex A): S/P for 10', 20' and 40' = 2787/5853/11985 × 2259 mm. Sources: ISO 668:2020, William Cook — ISO corner castings. In a container combination, each joint between two containers sits on one shared pile centred on the joint line, carrying the corner castings of both containers. With the default 200 mm flush heads the corner pile centres are 100 mm from the edges, within a few mm of the corner-casting aperture centres (102–103.5 mm from the ends, 89.5 mm from the sides). Intermediate supports (including the middle supports of the 20' field) are an editable starting point: before pouring concrete, check their contact with the longitudinal beams of the actual container (measure the gap with a feeler gauge with the container resting on its corner castings).
